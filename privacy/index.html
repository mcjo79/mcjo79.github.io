Privacy policy

How this site handles your data. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

This is the personal landing page of mcjo79. It presents open-source projects, starting with the VRoid MCP Bridge. The site has no account, no payment and no upload form.

Who is responsible?

The site is published by mcjo79, who acts as the data controller within the meaning of Article 4(7) GDPR. For any question or request about your personal data, contact the publisher through the project repository: github.com/mcjo79/mcjo79.github.io. If your request contains sensitive information, ask for a private channel first; GitHub issues are public.

Audience measurement

Only if you accept, Google Analytics 4 collects:

  • the pages you visit, the referring page and the links you click;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country, region or city);
  • technical information: device, operating system, browser, language and screen size;
  • interaction events such as scroll depth or outbound clicks;
  • a random identifier stored in a cookie to recognise a returning browser.

No name, email address or account is requested. No project file, model or image is sent to this site. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, according to Google: the IP address is used to derive the approximate location and then discarded.

If you decline, no Google Analytics script is loaded and no analytics request is made. Only your choice and theme preference are kept locally in your browser.

Cookies

No analytics cookie is set before you press Accept. After consent, Google Analytics 4 sets:

Analytics cookies are configured as session cookies: no persistent analytics identifier is kept after you close your browser.

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. Declining removes the analytics cookies set by this site. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Legal basis and consent

Audience measurement is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent is optional, is not a condition of access, and is collected by the banner. It is stored for 180 days, then the banner asks again. You may withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it.

The choice is stored under vroid-consent-v1 and applies to mcjo79.github.io and its project pages on the same domain, including the VRoid MCP Bridge site.

Recipients and transfers

Analytics data is processed by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA), acting as processors under Google's data processing terms. Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as applicable. See Google's privacy policy.

The site does not sell data, does not run advertising and does not build user profiles.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) through GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes technical connection data (IP address, browser, requested page) to deliver the site and keep it secure, on the basis of its legitimate interest in operating the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

How long is data kept?

Your consent choice is kept in your browser for 180 days. In Google Analytics 4, the user-data retention period is configured in the Analytics property (2 months by default, up to 14 months). Aggregated reports, which no longer identify a person, may be kept longer.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, to object, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact the publisher through the repository link above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In France, this is the CNIL; elsewhere in the EU/EEA, contact your national authority.

Local storage

Two values are stored in your browser's localStorage: mcjo79-theme (light or dark theme) and vroid-consent-v1 (your analytics choice and its expiry date). They are strictly necessary to remember your preferences and are never sent to a server.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. If a change affects your consent, the banner will ask again.
